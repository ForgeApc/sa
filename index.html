FAQ

Good to know

Is there really no key system?

Correct — Polluted Hub has no key system at all. There's no key page, no linkvertise step and nothing to renew. You paste the loader and the UI opens immediately.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. There's no premium tier and no locked modules — everything shown in the preview is available to everyone.

Which executors does it work with?

Any Lua executor that supports loadstring and HttpGet. The loader is tested against the most popular executors on each release.

How often are scripts updated?

Actively. Most supported games are repatched the same day an update breaks something, and every patch is announced in the Discord's #script-updates channel.

My game isn't supported — can you add it?

Request it in #game-requests on the Discord. Requests with the most votes get scripted first, and popular games usually land within a week or two.

Is it safe to use?

Using any third-party script is against Roblox's Terms of Service and carries a risk to your account. Polluted Hub's team works to keep scripts running smoothly, but no hub can promise zero risk — use it on an account you're willing to lose.
